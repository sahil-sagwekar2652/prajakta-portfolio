# Surface brief — homepage (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Experience (personal showcase) with Persuade discipline (one clear action). Scope: homepage with blog post cards; later surfaces: post pages, founder section expansion, photos page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Her name and the flower are one word. The homepage is a dawn garden floor strewn with fallen parijat: who she is leads, her stories lie along the path. It refuses the centred nameplate hero and the equal-card blog grid.

OWN-WORLD: dawn ivory ground, petal white, parijat saffron, deep leaf-green ink, bark-brown secondaries. Crisp vector parijat blossoms — slender white petals around a saffron tube — scattered sparsely as fallen flowers at margins and seams. Display face: Yatra One (hand-painted Mumbai signage voice; "yatra" = journey). Body/UI: Mukta. Self-hosted woff2, no hosted font CSS.

STORY: land in dawn light → her name and roles (athlete, trekker, poet, traveller, guide, writer, founder) → five real stories as editorial cards, latest largest → photo strip from her own photos → quiet Muhurta Yatra teaser with the venture logo → footer with Instagram, LinkedIn, Blogspot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: ivory field; blossoms fallen at margins; left-aligned Yatra One "Prajakta Patil" at display scale; role row in leaf green; one honest line in her voice; a large parijat sprig at right as the focal object; primary action "Read the latest trek" anchored to the featured card. No kicker, no button row, no stats.

FORM: brief-pinned world (parijat dawn, warm, very minimally Indian), confirmed 2026-10-13: identity-first structure, venture = small teaser, dawn scene. No concept roll — user-pinned direction beats the roll. Seed key: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
